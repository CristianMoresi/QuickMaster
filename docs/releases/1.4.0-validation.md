Public release description: analog saturation and tape machine presets, and a
hearing-based Auto EQ.

## Saturation acceptance

- `SaturationTrackAudit` on the packaged image, four private finished masters
  (By Now, Quiet Gold, Billie Jean (80s Glam Metal), Wicked Game (80s
  Synthwave)), every analog model and tape alignment at 1, 3 and 6 dB:
  **108 cases passed**. The meter never showed a gain and its deepest reading
  was the knob's to 0.0001 dB, measured against each model's linear response;
  loudness moved at most 0.044 dB; the sine knee was never needed; every second
  render was identical to the bit; every source file was unchanged. Details:
  [the saturation stage](../diagnostics/saturation-analog-stage.md).
- `ClipsUiAudit` on the packaged image: `CLIPS_UI_PASS`. The menu holds Soft
  clip, Tube preamp, Transformer, Multi-stage and Tape machine; published PCM
  equals a cold render to the bit for two curves and for 15 ips Colored.
- The regression reported on the first 1.4.0 build (Transformer and the 15 ips
  Colored, 7.5 ips Warm and 3.75 ips Vintage alignments saturating to the
  maximum at 1 dB, with a meter far past the knob) was reproduced on By Now,
  fixed and checked on the same track; the author listened to the installed
  build and accepted it.
